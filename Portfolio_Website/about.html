Life

Hi I'm Grant aka G. I grew up in a small farming town in California where I first became interested in programming. I hung out with my uncle playing games like Smash Brothers Brawl and Luigis Mansion that have had a grip on my life ever since. I at some point learned that programmers were the people that made such games and so that became the coolest career to me. At some point I was introduced to Scratch and some other teaching tools and at the time I simply could not care less. Little did I know at the time that coding really was what would interest me. I went through elementery school with no plans at all and highschool intending to become a cardiovascular perfusionist. After some time in college working towards that goal I found myself rather apathetic towards most of that health stuff. I found it interesting but hard to really engage with outside of chemistry. So I've since moved towards working for a dream job in a coding field. I've entered the coding space hoping to work as an indie game developer and while that is still my ultimate goal I've found that website design and programming is quite fun...
